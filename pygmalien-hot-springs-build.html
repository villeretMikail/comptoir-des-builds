Pygmalien Hot Springs build

1 run: 8 wins.

Open this build in the interactive tool

The core

  • Private Hot Springs

Variant 1: Ice Luge spam

With an Ice Cream Truck and an Ice Luge, especially with reduced Ammo, you can make good Freeze loops that trigger the Hot Springs reasonably fast.

What it adds:

  • Ice Luge · Engine: Cooldown reduction to spam the Hot Springs

Flex for this variant:

  • Ice Cream Truck: Charge loop with the Hot Springs

Example board: Private Hot Springs, Ice Luge, Ice Cream Truck, Stopwatch, Stopwatch, Icicle.

Flex

Items that fit the build's idea.

  • Badblocker: With a Freeze enchantment and lots of items over 10 value: high Multicast that charges the Hot Springs well
  • Stopwatch · Key: Big board-wide Freeze, good for the Springs
  • Icicle: Instant Charge at the start of combat
  • Steam Ladle: Benefits a lot from Freeze

How to play it

Look for items that Freeze quickly, ideally items that Charge each other to spam the Hot Springs. A Diamond Ice Luge, or 2 Ammo, can bring their cooldown down to 2s. Freeze enchantments on non-Freeze items can be good.

Freeze items that target many items are very good with Hot Springs.

Item roles

  • Engine: Almost always there, but replaceable.
  • Key: Rare, but when it shows up, you win.
