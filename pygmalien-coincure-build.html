The Bazaar · Pygmalien

Pygmalien Coincure build

Build up Coincure's value in combat (and out of combat if possible) so your weapons and Heal items get big bonuses. Having fast Heal and Damage items usually pays off.

Tested on patch 19.2 · 1 run: 10 wins.

These items are examples: no two games are alike, and endless variations are possible.

The core

  • Coincure

Variant 1: Toys

In the Toys variant, the goal is to find a Yo-Yo, then Toys that help it hit fast, so it gets the most out of the damage Coincure gives it. Fast Heal items are obviously essential, and a Heal enchantment on the Yo-Yo is an absolute banger. Piggles Board also seems to allow good damage and Heal loops if you get it.

Getting the Toy tag on Bandages or other Heal items is very good too.

What it adds:

  • Bandages · Engine
  • Kukri · Engine
  • Yo-Yo · Engine
  • Piggles Launcher · Engine

Flex for this variant:

  • Model Ship: Very good with Yo-Yo
  • Marbles: Very good now that Coincure also benefits from Slow
  • Haladie
  • Piggles Board: Charges the Toys off each other for more procs
  • Premium Green Piggles: Toy + fast Heal

Flex

Items that fit the build's idea.

  • Succulents
  • PenFT

How to play it

Look for fast Heal and Damage items; items that do both are excellent. A Heal enchantment on a Damage item (or the other way around) adds a lot of value. Items that add flat value can also fit in the build.

Kukri has both by default, so it's often excellent in this kind of build.

Item roles

  • Engine: Almost always there, but replaceable.
