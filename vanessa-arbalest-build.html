The core

  • Arbalest

Variant 1: Tools captain's quarter

Captain's Quarters Hastes all your Tools AND reloads. With enough Tools, you get Haste on many items at once, especially since plenty of Tools can Haste. It's a good way to combine Haste and reloading for the Arbalest.

What it adds:

  • Astrolabe
  • Dive Weights
  • Captain's Wheel · Engine
  • Captain's Quarters · Engine

Flex for this variant:

  • Beach Ball
  • Rowboat
  • Energy Potion

Example board: Astrolabe, Dive Weights, Captain's Quarters, Captain's Wheel, Arbalest.

How to play it

The Arbalest uses Haste to stack damage. The goal is to find a combination of items that generates lots of Haste, so it stacks as fast as possible and needs only a few hits to kill. There are several ways to get there, beyond the variants that will be added here: don't hesitate to try things.

Item roles

  • Engine: Almost always there, but replaceable.
