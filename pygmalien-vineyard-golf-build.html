The Bazaar · Pygmalien

Pygmalien Vineyard & Golf build

Vineyard and Golf Clubs are must-haves. The Bird and Bandages are almost always there but can be replaced. Bar of Soap, Textiles and Luxury Tents win games when they show up.

Stats: Bazaar DB, patch 19.0 (October 8 hotfix)

The core

  • Vineyard · Must-have
  • Golf Clubs · Must-have
  • Uwashiwali Bird · Engine
  • Bandages · Engine

Variant 1: The target build

Fast Heal items like Bar of Soap or Bandages, Multicast items like the Bird, or Textiles, which can charge the Golf Clubs quickly, all combine very well with this build. Luxury Tents double the Golf Clubs' scaling. Don't play Textiles with Luxury Tents if the Golf Clubs have a Heal enchantment, since Textiles don't charge Heal items.

What it adds:

  • Bar of Soap · Key
  • Textiles · Key: Starts at Gold: aim for it late in the run.
  • Luxury Tents · Key: Starts at Gold: aim for it late in the run.

Example board: Vineyard, Golf Clubs, Textiles, Uwashiwali Bird, Bandages, Bar of Soap.

Flex

Items that can slot into the build in place of another.

  • Fountain · Option
  • Succulents · Option
  • Lavomatique · Option

How to play it

The goal is to get the Vineyard and the Golf Clubs, then find sources of Heal (or Slow) that scale the Golf Clubs. The Lavomatique turns every Heal into a Slow and can potentially fit into any variant.

Item roles

  • Must-have: The build doesn't exist without it.
  • Engine: Almost always there, but replaceable.
  • Key: Rare, but when it shows up, you win.
  • Option: An option when there's room left.
