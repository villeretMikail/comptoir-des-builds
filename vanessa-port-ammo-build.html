Vanessa Port ammo build

Once you have the Port, you can go for Vanessa's usual Ammo items and try for an Ammo-spam run that hits hard.

How to play it

Port has the advantage of offering Ammo items from other heroes, and some of them Haste or Charge. Combined with the Torpedo, you can get single-weapon builds that hit hard very fast: Port charges the Torpedo, and the Torpedo scales naturally with all the Ammo items that trigger fairly quickly.

You can't always find Ammo items that Haste or Charge, so fast Ammo items can do the job too, especially with Pistol Sword, which benefits a lot from the other items spamming Ammo.

Item roles

  • Engine: Almost always there, but replaceable.
  • Key: Rare, but when it shows up, you win.
